## Summary

Preserve bounded meaning across structural scales

## Claim

### Prose Claim


recursive application of ANECHOIC meanings at structural scales.


a ANECHOIC invariant, claim, relation, **or** classification retains the same meaning **when** applied recursively at the Project root **or** **any** configured descendant structural scope. **every** use remains bounded **to** its declared scope **and** applicability; recursion does **not** widen authority, evidence, evaluation, **or** implementation coverage.
