# Axioms

Each number also appears in its linked source Atom. The Atoms contain the authoritative Claims.

## 1. Make growing Projects manageable

Operators and AI Agents can handle only part of a Project at once. Well-organized information lets the Project grow. Clear ownership tells us where to make a change. Good boundaries expose what the task needs, and the amount of structure should match the work.

### 1.1 Operators and AI Agents can only work with so much context at once

[AN-S-1484](../_anechoic_anechoic/AN-S-1484-SPEC-AXIOM--operators-and-ai-agents-can-only-work-with-so-much-context-at-once.md)

Operators have limited attention and working memory. AI Agents have limited context windows. A Project can hold more information than either can work with at once.

### 1.2 Information can be managed at any scale

[AN-S-1428](../_anechoic_anechoic/AN-S-1428-SPEC-AXIOM--information-can-be-managed-at-any-scale.md)

We can work efficiently with any amount of information if we organize it well and keep it organized as it changes.

### 1.3 Clear structure and ownership make information easier to maintain

[AN-S-1432](../_anechoic_anechoic/AN-S-1432-SPEC-AXIOM--clear-structure-and-ownership-make-information-easier-to-maintain.md)

Information is easier to maintain when we know what belongs where, what must be covered, and which source to change.

### 1.4 Internal complexity does not have to make a system difficult to use

[AN-S-1429](../_anechoic_anechoic/AN-S-1429-SPEC-AXIOM--internal-complexity-does-not-have-to-make-a-system-difficult-to-use.md)

A system can be complex inside without being equally complex to use. Good boundaries let users work with what matters to their task without having to understand every internal detail.

### 1.5 Complexity should match current needs

[AN-S-1437](../_anechoic_anechoic/AN-S-1437-SPEC-AXIOM--complexity-should-match-current-needs.md)

More structure, discipline, and checking are not always better. Their value depends on what the Project needs now.

## 2. Delegate and stay in control

The Operator can direct specialist work by relying on others’ expertise. Delegating judgment to AI Agents saves effort and makes outcomes less predictable. Seeing what happened, why it happened, and how the Project reached its current state helps the Operator stay in control.

### 2.1 Directing specialist work does not require mastering the craft

[AN-S-1448](../_anechoic_anechoic/AN-S-1448-SPEC-AXIOM--directing-specialist-work-does-not-require-mastering-the-craft.md)

A person can direct specialist work by relying on expertise provided by others, without mastering or performing that work personally.

### 2.2 Delegating judgment to AI Agents saves Operator effort but makes results less predictable

[AN-S-1473](../_anechoic_anechoic/AN-S-1473-SPEC-AXIOM--delegating-judgment-to-ai-agents-saves-operator-effort-but-makes-results-less-predictable.md)

When AI Agents decide on the Operator’s behalf, the Operator spends less effort making those decisions but has less certainty that the results will match their expectations.

### 2.3 Transparency supports informed control

[AN-S-1435](../_anechoic_anechoic/AN-S-1435-SPEC-AXIOM--transparency-supports-informed-control.md)

A system is easier to understand and control when we can see what is happening, what decisions and actions rely on, and how the current state came about.

## 3. Reduce work over the system’s life

A proven solution that fits saves work. Define the need, compare existing solutions, and build only what’s missing. Programs express computational rules that can automate repeated work. Fixing problems before they spread and keeping the parts consistent reduce effort over the system’s life.

### 3.1 Do not reinvent the wheel

[AN-S-1951](../_anechoic_anechoic/AN-S-1951-SPEC-AXIOM--do-not-reinvent-the-wheel.md)

A proven solution that fits the job saves us from solving the same problem again. When people and AI Agents already know how to use it, they also spend less time learning and explaining it.

### 3.2 Figure out what wheel you need then adopt the best one that fits

[AN-S-2810](../_anechoic_anechoic/AN-S-2810-SPEC-AXIOM--figure-out-what-wheel-you-need-then-adopt-the-best-one-that-fits.md)

Work out what would solve the problem before looking at existing solutions. Use that sketch to recognize the wheel when you find it, compare the versions, and build only what’s missing.

### 3.3 Programming is applicable math

[AN-S-4804](../_anechoic_anechoic/AN-S-4804-SPEC-AXIOM--programming-is-applicable-math.md)

Programming applies mathematics to define computation. Programs express rules that relate inputs and state to outputs, state changes, and effects.

### 3.4 Repeated work becomes more efficient through automation

[AN-S-1436](../_anechoic_anechoic/AN-S-1436-SPEC-AXIOM--repeated-work-becomes-more-efficient-through-automation.md)

Automating repeated work uses cheap computation to save Operator time and mental effort, LLM tokens, and time spent waiting for results.

### 3.5 Shift Left: Problems cost less when addressed before they spread

[AN-S-1434](../_anechoic_anechoic/AN-S-1434-SPEC-AXIOM--shift-left-problems-cost-less-when-addressed-before-they-spread.md)

Preventing a problem, or fixing it before other work depends on it, saves the effort of redoing that later work.

### 3.6 Maintaining the whole system pays off, especially in the long run

[AN-S-1431](../_anechoic_anechoic/AN-S-1431-SPEC-AXIOM--maintaining-the-whole-system-pays-off-especially-in-the-long-run.md)

Keeping a system’s parts consistent and working together reduces the time, effort, and money needed to keep it running over its life.
