## Summary

Replace CCE with ASD-STE100 and establish Atom authoring prompts

## Objective

Retire CCE as the Atom prose standard and adopt ASD-STE100-aligned prose. Establish separate Scope Units under AGENTIC/PROMPTS for an Atom authoring and update prompt and an autonomous Atom rewrite prompt.

## Work

1. Inventory CCE authority, references, validators and runtime consumers. Preserve independently required Role, Form, Term, Topic and relation semantics.
2. Replace the governing prose rules and reconcile affected Atoms and consumers.
3. Declare the two prompt Scope Units and their applicable specifications. Deliver and verify the prompts through their normal entry points.
4. Verify retirement coverage, prompt behavior and remaining limitations.

## Definition of Done

CCE-specific prose requirements are retired or reconciled. ASD-STE100-aligned authoring requirements are established. Both prompt Scope Units and their prompts are usable through the applicable project interfaces. Verified checks and any remaining work are recorded.
