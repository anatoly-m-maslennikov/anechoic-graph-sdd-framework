## Summary

Distinguish Scope Unit Declarations from observations

## Claim

An Evaluation fails when a consumer treats an observed folder or Goal as an authoritative Project or Component declaration, conceals a declared Component because its Carrier or active Goal is absent, or repairs contradictory sources without authorization. A missing Carrier or Goal does not erase a declaration or authorize a mutation.
