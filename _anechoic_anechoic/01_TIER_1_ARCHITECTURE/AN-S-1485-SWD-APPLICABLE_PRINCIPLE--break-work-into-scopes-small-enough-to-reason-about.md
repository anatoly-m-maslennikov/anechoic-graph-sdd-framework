**2.3**

## Summary

Break work into scopes small enough to reason about

## Claim

### Prose Claim

Give each task a clear boundary and keep its dependencies easy to find. That lets people and agents work on one part without losing how it fits into the whole.

## Details

### Examples

A team separates a data import change from a report layout change and records their shared data format. Each task has a clear boundary, and its dependency remains easy to find.
