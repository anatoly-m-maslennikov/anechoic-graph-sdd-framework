## Summary

Define Spec Atom template axes

## Claim

### Prose Claim

A Spec Atom template may preset independent axis values and constrain the allowed local Tier. Claim format is Formal or Prose. Every normative lens may use Declarative requirements stating what must hold and Imperative requirements giving a process or graph of actions; an Imperative process may call another process. A process can be programmatic, agentic, or hybrid. A Python Manifest uses Declarative control flow. Standards are mandatory; Conventions are advisory. The Project Manifest and Goal are Tier −1; Tier 1 Axioms state accepted truths and Tier 2 Applicable Principles guide choices.

## Details

Acceptance: a normative lens can contain both Declarative and Imperative requirements; a Python Manifest uses Declarative control flow. A Template may constrain its local Tier without making claim format or control flow identical to the Template. A Convention is advisory and a Standard is mandatory.
