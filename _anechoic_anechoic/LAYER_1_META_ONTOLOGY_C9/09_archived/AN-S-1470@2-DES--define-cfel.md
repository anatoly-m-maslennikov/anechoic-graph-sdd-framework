## Claim

CFEL (ANECHOIC Formal Expression Language) defines the syntax and meaning of graph expressions used in Subject addresses and Formal Claims.

CFEL is a model component alongside Graph and Carrier. The Graph contains Nodes and Relations; CFEL expresses them.
