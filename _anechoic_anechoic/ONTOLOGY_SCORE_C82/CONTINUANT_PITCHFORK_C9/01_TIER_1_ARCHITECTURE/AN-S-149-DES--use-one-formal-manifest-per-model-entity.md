## Claim

Each Node, Property, and Relation in the effective Model has exactly one Active Formal Manifest as its declaring source. Its Model address identifies what it declares. A separate Manifest can constrain the entity at its own address. Declare each new Relation meaning in its own Formal Manifest. A Relation name keeps its declared meaning.
