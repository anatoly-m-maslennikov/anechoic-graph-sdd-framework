## Summary

Define ANECHOIC Direct Route Skill

## Claim

### Prose Claim


ANECHOIC Direct Route Skills.


A ANECHOIC Direct Route Skill **must** be an Operator-defined Skill bound **to** **`=1`** branch **or** leaf registered **in** the ANECHOIC Routing Tree **and** **must** invoke **only** that bound target.
