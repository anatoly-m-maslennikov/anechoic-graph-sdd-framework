## Summary

Derive Scope Unit ancestry from Declarations

## Claim

Scope Unit ancestry is derived from the governing structural declarations and their direct parent relations: the Project Scope Unit Declaration and non-Project Scope Delegations. ANECHOIC does not author or persist a Structural Level property for a Scope Unit. A displayed path or depth is derived from that parent chain only. It does not establish or override parentage, ownership, or authority. Conflicting or unresolved parent inputs remain unresolved.
