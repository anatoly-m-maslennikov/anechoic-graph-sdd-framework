## Summary

Define Subject Path

## Claim

### Prose Claim

A Subject Path is the complete canonical expression for one Main Subject or Topic Dependency. Use the declared namespace, target name, and any qualification needed to identify it. A defining Atom can introduce the named target.

CA-R-1321 defines Subject notation and resolution. A display label, Scope Unit, or Carrier path cannot replace the canonical Subject Path.
