## Claim

Scope Unit ancestry **must** be derived from the authoritative Project Structure parent declarations and their corresponding stored structural-parent relations. ANECHOIC **must not** author or persist a Structural Level property for a Scope Unit. A displayed ancestry depth or path segment, **when** materialized for readability, is derived from that parent chain only; it **must not** establish, replace, or override parentage, ownership, or authority, and unresolved or conflicting inputs remain unresolved.
