## Summary

Revise Current specification and Task Atoms through same-ID Drafts

## Claim

### Prose Claim

A content or authored-property change to a Current SPEC or PMETA Atom, or a Current Ticket such as a Task must be made in a Draft revision with the same Atom ID and reviewed before promotion.

## Details

Acceptance: A Current SPEC Atom A is changed in Draft A, then reviewed before promotion.

A Current Task T is changed through a Draft of T, not by editing Current T in place.
