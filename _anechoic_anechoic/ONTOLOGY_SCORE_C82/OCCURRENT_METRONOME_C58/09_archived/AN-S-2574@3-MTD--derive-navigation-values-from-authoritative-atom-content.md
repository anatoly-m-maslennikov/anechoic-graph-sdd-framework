## Summary

Derive navigation values from authoritative Atom content

## Claim

### Prose Claim

Derive an Atom's navigation values from authoritative content rather than another navigation value. For a new Atom, derive the Summary from its finalized primary contribution within its applicability. For RMED, use the Claim read within Scope, not Scope alone. Choose clear wording without adding authoritative meaning.

Use the complete applicable source content and its restrictions when validating or reconstructing the Atom’s content or deriving a Translation or other Projection. Use the Summary for navigation. For an existing Atom identity, retain its Summary and check source faithfulness. A needed Summary change follows the Atom identity rule; do not regenerate the Summary as an independent Projection.
