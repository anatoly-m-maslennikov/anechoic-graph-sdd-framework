## Summary

Write clear Atom content and use canonical entity names

## Claim

### Prose Claim

When you write a Claim, name each necessary participant and relation. State each necessary modality, quantity, condition, and boundary. Do not use ambiguous pronouns, anaphora, ellipsis, unstated defaults, or mixed logical groupings. Make the meaning, conditions, and consequences clear.

In authored content, use each entity's exact admitted canonical name and use each governed Term with its authoritative meaning. Resolve the entity and its name before you mention it. Do not infer identity from spelling or substitute a synonym for an admitted name. If a required entity or Term binding is missing, ambiguous, or conflicting, report the uncertainty without inventing a name or meaning.

Apply the Atom's declared Template. Check its primary contribution and supporting sections against that Template. Report missing or conflicting authority with the affected contribution.

For a declarative Spec, name the governed entity or required result and state its observable boundaries. Put operative steps in the applicable procedure.

For a procedure, identify its purpose, applicable inputs, choices, dependencies, output, and any unresolved decision. Reference independently governed constraints and checks.

For a Carrier rule, name the represented entity and state the applicable format, content, address, and placement. Use the declared Relation for a representation binding.

For a planning Ticket, state the intended action, object, result, and boundary. Record its assignee when known. A Ticket satisfied entirely through decomposition states the outcome and identifies its children. Keep Details within that outcome and write the Definition of Done under AN-S-2306.
