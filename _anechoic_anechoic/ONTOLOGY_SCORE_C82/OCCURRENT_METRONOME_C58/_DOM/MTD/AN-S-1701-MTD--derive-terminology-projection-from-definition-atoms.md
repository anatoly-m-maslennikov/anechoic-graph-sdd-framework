## Summary

Derive Terminology Projection from Definition Atoms

## Claim

### Prose Claim


optional derivation of a terminology Projection from active Method Atoms of Type Term Definition.


**to** build a terminology list using the Catalog Type, the Generator **must** derive **every** Governed Term's name **and** Project-specific meaning from its active Method Atom of Type Term Definition. Use the Term named in the defining Claim and record the exact source Revision. inspect **every** named entity **and** named component of a Subject Path, **not** **only** its terminal component; extract **only** evidenced occurrences of reusable Project Terms. keep exact full entity names **and** source-admitted instance labels distinct from reusable Term occurrences; their spelling **or** position **in** a Subject Path alone does **not** classify them as Terms **or** require a Term Definition. obtain **every** evidenced reusable Term's meaning from that Term's own defining Claim, **not** by treating **all** path components as definitions supplied by the referencing Atom **or** inferring its meaning from Entity Definitions **or** their Semantics. include an entry **only** **when** its explicit Term Definition Claim establishes a Project-specific meaning; consistent use **or** capitalization alone does **not** supply defining authority. report uncertain Term classification **or** segmentation as unresolved; **do not** invent a definition, infer lexical authority from spelling, **or** silently resolve that uncertainty as ordinary vocabulary. exclude words **and** phrases used **only** with their ordinary English meanings. retain the source reference **without** classifying the direct Subject reference **or** a complete composite Subject Path as a Term **or** creating independent vocabulary authority.

## Details

This Method applies only when a terminology Projection is requested. It does not make a dictionary, lookup table, Terms Graph, or other Projection a prerequisite for direct source-based authoring or evaluation. Lexical meaning ownership remains with the exact active Term Definition Atom and revision; source selection does not turn an Entity Definition or derived Projection into lexical defining authority.
