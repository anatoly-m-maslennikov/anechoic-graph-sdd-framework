## Summary

Attribute each governed action to its Actor

## Claim

### Prose Claim

Before a governed action, identify its initiating Actor and the approval or active delegation that authorizes it.

An AI Agent acting without per-action approval must check that an active Operator-issued delegation covers the Agent, action, target Scope, and constraints. Verify the validity boundary and revocation state.
