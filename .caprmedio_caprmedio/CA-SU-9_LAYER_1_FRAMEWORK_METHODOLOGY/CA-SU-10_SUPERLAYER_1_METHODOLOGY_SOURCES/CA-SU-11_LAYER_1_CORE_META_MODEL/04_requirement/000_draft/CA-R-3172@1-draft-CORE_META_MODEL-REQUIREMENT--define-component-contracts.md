## Summary

Define Component Contracts

## Claim

### Prose Claim

A Contract states a concrete guarantee. When a child Component has a Purpose, its Contracts collectively meet that Purpose. Child Component Contracts are Tier 1. A consumer may record a need as an optional Demand, and a producer Contract can fulfill it. At the Project root, use a Contract for a concrete exposed guarantee; Applicable Principles independently guide decisions.

## Details

Acceptance: a Project Applicable Principle can stand without a Contract; a concrete exposed guarantee is represented by a Contract.
