## Summary

Preserve canonical spelling and exact reference case

## Claim

### Prose Claim

When you author CAPRMEDIO content, capitalize Governed Terms and preserve the canonical spelling and required case of Terms and Scope Unit Names. Preserve the exact spelling and case of registered references. Apply these rules at the start of a sentence or list item as well as elsewhere. Follow any other applicable rule that requires an exact-case token.
