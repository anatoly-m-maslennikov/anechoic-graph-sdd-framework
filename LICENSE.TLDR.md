# Anechoic license in plain language

This page explains the [Anechoic license](LICENSE) in plain language.
Anechoic is source-available under Apache 2.0 with
[Commons Clause 1.0](https://commonsclause.com/) and a public-hosting condition.

## Free to use, change, and share

- Use or self-host Anechoic for personal, nonprofit, or business work.
- Change it and publish your fork for free. Keep the license and copyright
  notices, and mark what you changed.
- Use Anechoic to build your own products and sell those products.

## When you need an agreement

Contact [Anatoly Maslennikov](https://github.com/anatoly-m-maslennikov)
for a written agreement before selling Anechoic or charging for access
to it. This includes paid downloads, self-hosted packages you sell to
customers, and SaaS. The same rule applies to a paid fork if most of
its value still comes from Anechoic.

Get a written agreement before offering the public a hosted service
that gives users access to a substantial part of Anechoic or a fork.
This applies even if access is free.

Paid consulting and support can also require an agreement if most of
the value comes from Anechoic.

Third-party components keep their own licenses.
Versions already received under Apache 2.0 keep those permissions.
