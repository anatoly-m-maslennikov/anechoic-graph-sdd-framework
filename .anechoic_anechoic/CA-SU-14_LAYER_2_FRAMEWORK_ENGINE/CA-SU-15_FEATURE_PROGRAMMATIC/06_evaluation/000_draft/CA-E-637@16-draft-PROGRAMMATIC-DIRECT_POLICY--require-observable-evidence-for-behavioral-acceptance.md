## Summary

Require observable evidence for behavioral acceptance

## Claim

### Prose Claim

For PROGRAMMATIC behavior, require one or more reproducible checks per
declared failure predicate. Acceptance cannot rely only on replaceable
private structure when a semantic or public boundary can expose the predicate.

## Details

- This policy establishes evidence needed for acceptance. Portfolio
  selection, cost ranking, and test-scope ratios are separate decisions.
