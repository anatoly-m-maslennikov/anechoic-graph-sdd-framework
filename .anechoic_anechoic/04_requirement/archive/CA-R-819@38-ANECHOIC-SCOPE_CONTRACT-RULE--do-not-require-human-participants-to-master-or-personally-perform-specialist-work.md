## Summary

Do not require human participants to master or personally perform specialist work

## Claim

### Prose Claim


the ANECHOIC Framework Instance **must not** require **any** of the Operator's human participants **to** acquire specialist craft proficiency **or** personally perform specialist craft work **to** create, deliver, run, **or** maintain **any** feasible Project.
