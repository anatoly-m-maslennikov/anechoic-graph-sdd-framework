## Summary

Calibrate guidance to current adequacy

## Claim

### Prose Claim

ANECHOIC Skills **and** their prompts provide **only** the guidance necessary **to** meet the operator-defined adequacy threshold under the current model, provider, host, task, risk, **and** Evaluation conditions, increasing **or** reducing guidance **when** evidence changes those conditions.
